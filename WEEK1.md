# Week 1 - Planning & Reconnaissance

## Engagement Planning
- Defined red team engagement rules of engagement  
- Identified attack scenarios and threat actors to emulate  
- Established communication protocols with blue team  
- Set success criteria and key performance indicators  

## Reconnaissance
- Performed passive and active reconnaissance on target environment  
- Mapped system architecture and network topology  
- Identified potential entry points and exposed services  
- Gathered open-source intelligence (OSINT) on assets
